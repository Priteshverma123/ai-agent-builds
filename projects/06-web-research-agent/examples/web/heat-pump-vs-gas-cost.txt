Title: Heat pump vs gas furnace: what it really costs
Type: news

A whole-home heat pump installation cost averaged 14,000 USD in 2025, against about 6,000 USD for a new gas furnace. In cold regions, annual heating bills with a heat pump were 20 to 40 percent lower than with gas, depending on local electricity prices. The payback period for a heat pump ranged from 7 to 12 years before rebates. Where electricity is expensive and gas is cheap, savings can shrink to almost nothing.
