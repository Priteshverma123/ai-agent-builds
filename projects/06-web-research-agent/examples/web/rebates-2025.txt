Title: Heat pump rebates expand for cold states
Type: news

Federal and state programs covered up to 8,000 USD of heat pump installation cost in 2025. Several cold states added extra rebates for certified cold-climate heat pump models. With rebates, the typical payback for a cold climate heat pump fell to 4 to 7 years.
