# 3 days in Paris on a budget

Paris rewards slow travellers who walk everywhere and eat where the locals eat. Start day one in the Marais before the crowds arrive, then cross to the islands for the cathedral views. The Louvre is free on the first Friday evening of each month, so plan your trip around it if you can.

Day two belongs to Montmartre: climb to Sacre-Coeur at sunrise and have coffee on a quiet side street.
