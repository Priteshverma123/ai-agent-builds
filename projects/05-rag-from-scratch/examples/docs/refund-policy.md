# Refunds and returns

Unopened bags of coffee can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bags can't be returned. If you didn't enjoy a roast, contact us within 14 days and we will send you a different roast for free.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. Return shipping is free for customers in the United States.
