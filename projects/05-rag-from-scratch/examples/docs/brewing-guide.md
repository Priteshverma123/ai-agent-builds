# Brewing guide

For pour-over, use 15 grams of coffee to 250 grams of water at about 94 degrees Celsius. Pour slowly in circles and aim for a total brew time of about three minutes.

For a French press, use a coarse grind and steep for four minutes before pressing.

Store beans in an airtight container away from light. Use them within four weeks of the roast date for the best flavour.
