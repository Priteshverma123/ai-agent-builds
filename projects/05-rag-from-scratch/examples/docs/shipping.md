# Shipping

We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and ship within 24 hours of roasting. Standard shipping in the United States takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $35.

We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom. International delivery takes 7 to 12 business days and costs a flat $15. We do not currently ship to Australia.

Every order gets a tracking link by email as soon as the shipping label is printed.
