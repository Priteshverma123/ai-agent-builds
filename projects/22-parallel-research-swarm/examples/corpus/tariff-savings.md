title: Do batteries pay off on time-of-use tariffs?
url: local://energyhub.example/tou-savings
latency_ms: 100
---
On a time-of-use tariff, a battery can save $400 to $900 per year on the electricity bill.
Payback periods of 8 to 12 years are common without a rebate.
Without solar panels, savings come only from shifting cheap night energy to the evening peak.
