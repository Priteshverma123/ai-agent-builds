LFP cells are far less prone to thermal runaway than NMC cells.
Thermal runaway is the chain reaction behind most battery fire incidents.
LFP batteries keep about 80 percent of their capacity after 6,000 cycles.
NMC batteries are lighter but degrade faster with daily cycling.
