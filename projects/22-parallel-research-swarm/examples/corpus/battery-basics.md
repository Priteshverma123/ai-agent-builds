A typical 10 kWh home battery costs between $8,000 and $14,000 installed in 2026.
Most home batteries today use lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry.
A battery stores cheap solar or off-peak energy and releases it in the evening.
Manufacturers usually offer a 10-year warranty on the battery.
