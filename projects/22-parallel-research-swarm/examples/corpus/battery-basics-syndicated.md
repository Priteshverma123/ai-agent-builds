A typical 10 kWh home battery costs between $8,000 and $14,000 installed, as of 2026.
Most home batteries today use lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry, according to installers.
Manufacturers usually offer a 10-year warranty on the battery itself.
Readers should compare at least three quotes before signing.
