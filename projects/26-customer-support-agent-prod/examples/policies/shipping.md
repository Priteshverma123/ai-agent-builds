# Shipping policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships. Express shipping takes 1 to 2
business days.

## Tracking
Every shipped order has a tracking number. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after
the carrier scans a parcel.

## Lost or late parcels
If a parcel is more than 5 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact support and we
will open a carrier investigation and send a replacement or a refund.
