# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Refunds apply to delivered orders. Orders that are still processing or in transit can be cancelled
instead; cancellation is handled by the orders team.

## Refund window
How long do you have? You can return an item and request a refund within 30 days of delivery.
After 30 days we cannot refund the order, but items may still be covered by the manufacturer warranty.

## Final sale items
Items marked final sale (for example clearance products) are not refundable and cannot be returned.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. You can request a full
or a partial refund, up to the amount you paid minus anything already refunded.

## Approvals
Refunds above 200 USD are reviewed by a support specialist before money is sent. Customers are told
that the request is pending and usually hear back within one business day.
