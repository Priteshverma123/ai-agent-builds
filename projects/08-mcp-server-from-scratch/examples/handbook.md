# Team handbook

## Refunds
Customers can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days we offer store credit only.
Refunds above $500 need a manager's approval.

## On-call
The on-call engineer answers pages within 15 minutes. Hand over every Monday at 10:00.

## Releases
We release on Thursdays. No releases on Fridays or before public holidays.
