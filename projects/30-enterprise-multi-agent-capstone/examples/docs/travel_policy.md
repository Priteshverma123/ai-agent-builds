# Travel Policy

## Flights
Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and needs written VP approval before booking.

## Trains and ground transport
Prefer trains for trips under 4 hours. Taxis and ride-shares are reimbursed for airport transfers and late-night travel after 21:00.

## Safety
Register every international trip in the travel portal at least 7 days before departure so the security team can reach you.
