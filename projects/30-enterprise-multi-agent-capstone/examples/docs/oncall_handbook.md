# On-call Handbook

## Response times
A SEV1 page must be acknowledged within 15 minutes, 24/7. SEV2 pages must be acknowledged within 30 minutes during business hours. SEV3 issues are handled in the next sprint.

## Handover
On-call rotations change every Monday at 10:00 local time. The outgoing engineer writes a handover note listing open incidents and risky changes.

## Escalation
If the primary on-call does not acknowledge within the response time, the page escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.
