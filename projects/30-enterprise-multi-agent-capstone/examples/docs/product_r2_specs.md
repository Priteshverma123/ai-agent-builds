# R2 Warehouse Robot Specifications

## Payload and speed
The R2 robot carries a maximum payload of 12 kg and moves at up to 2.5 metres per second on flat floors.

## Battery
The R2 battery lasts 9 hours per charge and fully recharges in 90 minutes. A swollen or hot battery must be removed from service immediately and reported to Ops.

## Safety
R2 stops within 0.4 seconds when its lidar detects a person closer than 1 metre.
