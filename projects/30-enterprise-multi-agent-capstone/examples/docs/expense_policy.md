# Expense Policy

## Meals
The meal per diem is $60 per day when travelling for work. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Keep itemised receipts for any single meal above $25.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in tier-1 cities (Berlin, London, New York, Tokyo) and up to $130 per night everywhere else. Book through the travel portal so the negotiated rate applies.

## Submitting claims
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip in the finance portal. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.
